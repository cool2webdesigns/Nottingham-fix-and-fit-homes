Frequently Asked Questions

Are your prices fixed?

Prices are starting guides. We provide a quotation after understanding the work, measurements and materials required.

Can I request a painting or plastering quote?

Yes. Tell us the room or wall size, condition and type of work required. Photographs can help us assess the job.

Do you build partition walls?

We can consider suitable non-structural partition projects. Structural alterations require appropriate professional assessment and approvals.

Can I send photographs?

Yes. Open WhatsApp using the enquiry form and attach your photographs to the message.

Do you work for landlords?

We welcome enquiries for suitable small property maintenance jobs. Contact us for a quotation.
